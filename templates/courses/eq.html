Emotional Wellness Rooted in Faith

Emotional Intelligence is the ability to understand, manage, and express your emotions while navigating relationships with empathy and wisdom. In Islam, self-mastery and beautiful character (akhlaq) are among the highest goals of a believer.

This 12-month program blends practical EQ techniques with Quranic guidance and Prophetic wisdom — helping you pause before reacting, handle difficult feelings with grace, and build healthier relationships with your family, friends, and community.

Emotion Regulation

Thoughtful Responses
