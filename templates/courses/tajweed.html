What You'll Master

Comprehensive Tajweed training from fundamentals to advanced rules

Makharij (Articulation Points)

Learn exactly where each letter originates in the mouth, throat, and chest

Sifat (Characteristics)

Understand the unique qualities of each letter for perfect pronunciation

Nooun Sakin & Tanween

Master the rules of Nooun Sakin and the three types of Tanween

Idghaam & Ikhfaa

Learn when to merge, hide, or pronounce letters clearly

Madd (Elongation)

Perfect timing for natural, extended, and required elongations
